ℹ️ About HealthLink Malawi

HealthLink Malawi is a digital health communication platform developed for the Blantyre District Health Office (DHO) to bridge communication gaps between communities, health workers, and district health administration.

Key Features:

  • Multi-channel access (USSD, SMS, WhatsApp, Web)
  • Real-time patient feedback system
  • Health worker operational reporting
  • Public health alert broadcasting
  • Data-driven dashboard for decision making
